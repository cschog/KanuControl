used, modified and redistributed under that license. Third-party components
remain subject to their own licenses.

Section 6 of the Apache License does not grant general permission to use
the licensor's trade names, trademarks, service marks or product names.
It expressly preserves reasonable and customary use to describe the origin
of the work and to reproduce the NOTICE file.

## Independent or modified distributions

Without explicit written authorization, do not use the KanuControl name,
logo or icon as the product branding of an independently maintained,
modified or redistributed version. Use your own product name and replace
the visible KanuControl branding in such a distribution.

Do not present a derivative version as an official KanuControl release or
imply endorsement by Chris Schog without authorization.

## Attribution and required notices

Replacing product branding does not mean removing required attribution.
Retain copyright, license, applicable trademark and attribution notices as
required by Section 4 of the Apache License, including the applicable
contents of NOTICE.md.

Reasonable and customary references identifying KanuControl as the origin
of the software, and reproduction of the NOTICE file, remain permitted as
specified in Section 6. Such attribution is not an assertion of official
endorsement and does not authorize using the project logo as your product
branding.
